# Fantasy Premier League

## Official Rules

### Top 5 & Bottom 5 Predictions

- **Exact position:** 3 points
- **Correct area:** 1 point if the team finishes somewhere in the predicted area (Top 5 or Bottom 5), but not in the exact predicted position

*A team cannot receive both the exact-position and area points for the same prediction.*

### Mystery Pick

- Predict **1 team to finish 6th–15th**
- **Exact position:** 5 points

*No points are awarded if the team finishes outside 6th–15th or in a different position.*

### Special Picks

Each Special Pick is worth **3 points** if the prediction is correct.

- **Most Goals:** Team with the most goals scored
- **Fewest Goals:** Team with the fewest goals scored
- **Fewest Goals Conceded:** Team with the fewest goals conceded
- **Most Goals Conceded:** Team with the most goals conceded
- **Best Goal Difference:** Team with the highest goal difference
- **Worst Goal Difference:** Team with the lowest goal difference
- **Closest Goal Difference to 0:** Team whose goal difference is closest to zero
- **Most Wins:** Team with the most wins
- **Most Draws:** Team with the most draws
- **Most Losses:** Team with the most losses
- **Most Clean Sheets:** Team with the most clean sheets

All Special Picks are determined using the **final Premier League standings and completed league matches** at the end of the season.

### Ties

If multiple teams are tied for a Special Pick, the prediction is considered correct if the selected team is **one of the tied teams**.

For example, if two teams finish with the same number of clean sheets and you selected either one, you receive the 3 points.

## Total Points

Your final score is the combined total of:

- Top 5 & Bottom 5 Predictions
- Mystery Pick
- Special Picks

The player with the **most points at the end of the season wins the league**.
